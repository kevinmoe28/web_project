Who are we?

We are a small company headquartered in western Yangon, Myanmar. We try our best to satisfy everyone's needs and wants, while maintaining the three principles listed below:

1. No delays

When our customers order a product or more, we always make sure the products arrive on time and no later. We compensate if we accidentally do.

2. Accept complaints

If customers find somthing unsatisfactory and go on to complain, we humbly listen to them; complaints to us are a method of finding ways to improve ourselves.

3. Legitimacy

We use the profits we make to make improvements to our services only; we never intend to do unfair or illegal actions, such as raising prices for no particular reason, or sending money to groups that involve crime.
